## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
### We propose an AI-powered digital marketplace that directly connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) with consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers.
### Direct buying and selling without unnecessary intermediaries.
### Farmers can list their products, quantities, and prices.
### Consumers can compare prices and purchase fresh products.
### AI-based demand prediction helps farmers understand market requirements.
### A transparent pricing system ensures fair transactions.
### A delivery management system connects farmers with logistics providers.
### Innovation: The platform combines direct agricultural trading, AI-based demand forecasting, and smart logistics in one digital solution.

## Technical Approach
### The platform will be developed using modern web technologies and artificial intelligence.
### Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React.js.
### Backend: Python and Django.
### Database: MySQL or PostgreSQL.
### AI/ML: Python, Scikit-learn for demand forecasting.
### Maps and Routes: Google Maps API for route optimization.
### Authentication: Secure login for farmers, buyers, and administrators.
### Payment: Integration with a secure digital payment gateway.
### Working Process:
### Farmers register and upload their agricultural products.
### Buyers search and select available products.
### The system matches farmers with buyers.
### AI predicts demand and recommends suitable quantities.
### Logistics providers handle product delivery.
### Buyers receive products directly from farmers.

## Feasibility and Viability
### The proposed platform provides economic, social, and environmental benefits.
### For Farmers:
### Increased income through direct selling.
### Better market access.
### Reduced dependency on intermediaries.
### Fair and transparent pricing.
### For Consumers:
### Fresh agricultural products.
### Lower purchasing costs.
### Transparent product prices.
### Direct access to farmers.
### For Society and Environment:
### Reduced food wastage.
### Improved agricultural supply chain.
### Better transportation planning.
### Support for rural economic development.

## Impact and Benefits
### The proposed solution is technically and economically feasible because it uses existing technologies and digital platforms.
### Feasibility:
### Can be developed using open-source technologies.
### Supports both mobile phones and computers.
### Can be implemented in rural and urban areas.
### Can be expanded to support multiple languages.
### Potential Challenges:
### Limited internet access in rural areas.
### Farmers may have limited digital knowledge.
### Transportation and storage difficulties.
### Maintaining product quality during delivery.
### Solutions:
### Provide a simple, multilingual user interface.
### Conduct farmer awareness and training programs.
### Partner with local transport and storage providers.
### Introduce product quality verification.
